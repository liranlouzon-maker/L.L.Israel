Physical Security Advice for People, Sites and Events

Personal and Executive Protection Planning

Assess personal exposure, schedules, destinations and the level of protective support required by executives, families and delegations. Coordinate protection requirements with appropriate local providers where needed.

Site Security Surveys and Residential Reviews

Review physical access, visitor arrangements, existing personnel and procedures at residences, business premises and other fixed sites. Identify practical changes that fit how the property is used.

Physical Security Risk Assessments

Identify threats and vulnerabilities affecting people and property, then set priorities for reducing exposure. Background checks and due diligence can support an agreed assessment or provider-selection requirement.

Event Security Planning

Define security requirements around a venue, audience, event schedule, access arrangements and arrivals. Coordinate responsibilities with organizers and suitable local providers.

What a Consulting Engagement Can Include

The scope is agreed for each project. Depending on the requirement, work can include an information review, a site survey, an assessment of current arrangements, practical recommendations, operating procedures or support coordinating implementation.

A remote review and an on-site survey provide different levels of information. We agree the appropriate approach before starting, including any local-provider involvement and the expected outputs.
