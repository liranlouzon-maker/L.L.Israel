Questions About International Physical Security Consulting

What does a physical security risk assessment cover?

An assessment examines threats and vulnerabilities affecting people, premises or an event. Depending on the agreed scope, it can review access arrangements, routines, existing security measures and responsibilities, then prioritize practical recommendations.

Can security consulting be carried out remotely?

A remote review can examine documents, plans and information supplied by the client. An on-site survey allows direct observation of the property and its routines. We agree the appropriate approach and its limitations before work begins.

What can I receive at the end of a consulting project?

Depending on the agreed scope, outputs can include findings, prioritized recommendations, an action plan, operating procedures or a brief for coordinating implementation. The deliverables and responsibilities are agreed for each project.

Do you provide physical security services in every European country?

Availability and delivery arrangements are reviewed for each destination. On-site protection and other local services depend on the applicable requirements and suitable local providers. LLISR does not claim an office or licensed operation in every country.
