Security Services in Israel

Executive and Close Protection

Discreet protection for corporate executives, senior officials, high-profile individuals, families and private clients. Coverage is planned around the client’s schedule, public exposure, destinations and preferred level of visibility.

Secure Transportation

Coordination of professional drivers, security drivers, airport transfers, suitable vehicles and armored vehicles upon request. Transportation and protection can be managed as one continuous operation.

Delegation and Corporate Support

Security and logistics for business delegations, corporate groups, events and visits involving multiple locations or providers. One dedicated contact coordinates the moving parts and keeps the plan consistent.

Risk Consulting

Risk assessments, security surveys, route and venue reviews, travel-security advice, background checks and due diligence. Recommendations are practical, proportionate and matched to the client’s operating environment.

An Israeli Security Approach

Israeli security experience is associated with preparation, adaptability and close attention to changing conditions. Our approach begins with understanding the person, mission and environment. We then connect intelligence, planning, protection, transportation and logistics into one coordinated solution.

This model is especially useful for overseas clients who need a trusted local partner in Israel. Instead of managing separate providers, the client works through one point of contact who coordinates the required services from planning through execution.

Support for US and European Clients

International clients often require more than a security officer. They may need airport assistance, secure transportation, schedule coordination, hotel and venue arrangements, local recommendations, privacy and the ability to adapt quickly. LLISR can combine these elements into one tailored operational plan in Israel and, through professional colleagues and partners, in additional countries.

Services are available for short visits, business travel, delegations, family trips, events and longer assignments. Every request is reviewed individually and remains subject to local requirements and operational availability.
