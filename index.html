Accessibility Statement

הצהרת נגישות

LLISR is committed to making its website accessible in accordance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines and applicable accessibility standards.

Accessibility Features

  • Fully keyboard-navigable
  • Compatible with screen readers (NVDA, JAWS and similar)
  • Text size adjustment (increase/decrease)
  • High contrast, inverted colors, and greyscale modes
  • Link and heading highlighting
  • Large cursor mode
  • Animation pause
  • Line spacing and letter spacing adjustment
  • Readable font option

Known Limitations

Some content areas may not yet be fully accessible. We are continuously working to improve accessibility across the site.

Accessibility Widget Disclaimer

The accessibility widget available on this site is a supplementary tool only and does not substitute for accessible development. It does not provide legal protection or constitute a certification of full accessibility.

התאמות הנגישות באתר

  • האתר מותאם לשימוש באמצעות מקלדת בלבד

  • ניתן להגדיל ריווח שורות וריווח אותיות
  • ניתן לעבור לפונט קריא

רכיבים שאינם נגישים במלואם
