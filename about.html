About LLISR

About Us

LLISR is an Israel-based consulting and security company focused on physical security and professional, personal service in Israel, Europe and internationally. Our team is drawn from the highest levels of Israel’s security, military and law-enforcement systems and combines operational experience with discreet, personal service. We support businesses, executives, delegations, families and private clients from the United States, Europe and other international markets.

Our services include the planning, coordination, and management of services and projects; identifying and connecting clients with the providers best suited to their needs; conducting reliability and due diligence checks; and providing professional guidance from planning through execution. Our experience, professional network, and commitment to personal service enable us to deliver professional, discreet, and efficient solutions while saving clients time, resources, and costs.

Accessibility Statement

הצהרת נגישות

LLISR is committed to making its website accessible in accordance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines and applicable accessibility standards.

Accessibility Features

  • Fully keyboard-navigable
  • Compatible with screen readers (NVDA, JAWS and similar)
  • Text size adjustment (increase/decrease)
  • High contrast, inverted colors, and greyscale modes
  • Link and heading highlighting
  • Large cursor mode
  • Animation pause
  • Line spacing and letter spacing adjustment
  • Readable font option
